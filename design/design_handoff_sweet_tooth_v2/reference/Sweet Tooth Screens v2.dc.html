Approach
The shack is a place: the HUD holds day, phase, coins and the phase button in one bar. The stage strip is one line of seven day pips; the rival warning is a chip that opens its hint. Errands collapse to a row with Accept; the description opens on tap.
Built for motion later
Shelf, cauldron, customer, clerk and upgrade are self-contained blocks with states. Patience is a segmented meter that can become continuous; the cauldron slot sits in a scene panel that can hold steam and stirring.
