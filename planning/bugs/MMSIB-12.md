# MMSIB-12 — Playtest batch 2: dead honey upgrade, contract button, wording, alternates too early

**Reported (playtest after MMSIM-12):** (1) The first upgrade, more honey, did nothing: 3 batches a day use 3 honey, so 7 units went unused. (2) Queen's quest and pilgrim's feast asked for the same candy. (3) Done quests could not be opened to re-read the reward. (4) Contracts said "by day 16" (not a number the player sees) and "Deliver" was enabled without enough stock; they also asked for an exact quality when a better one would do. (5) Royal jelly was on the shelf long before Perfect could be made. (6) The strip counted stages as weeks.

**Root cause:** (1) Plain costs 1 honey a batch and batches a day are capped, so an extra unit had no use until the oven; that broke the "first upgrade must matter" rule of the design. (4) The contract button had no disabled state; deadlines were the global day number. (5) The queen's reward granted royal jelly, the Perfect quality only comes with the magic cauldron. (6) "week N" was the stage number.

**Fix:** (1) Unused base ingredients are sold when the shop closes (`surplusValue`, 1 coin each for honey and sunflower seed): the shelf says so, the evening report shows "Leftovers sold", leftovers count as revenue. Alternates are never sold and now carry over to the next day, so the rare ones are not wasted. (2) Pilgrim's feast asks for 8 Fine sunflower brittles. (3) Done errands are expandable like the others. (4) Contract and errand lines say "Plain or better" (it always accepted better stock) and "by week 2, day 3" (calendar week and day of the week, which line up with the 7 day stages); the Deliver button is disabled until enough candy of that quality or better is in stock. (5) Royal jelly now comes from a new errand, "The hive's gratitude" (8 Fine sunflower nougats), which needs the magic cauldron; the dark seed garden upgrade needs it too (`needs` on a node: unlock ids). The queen's reward keeps the honey and the bees. (6) The strip reads "stage 2 · week 5 · day 3 / 7"; stage 1 is now called "The first steps" / "Les premiers pas" since a stage can take several weeks.

**Verified:** `npm run check`, `npm test` (181), `npm run balance` (smart bot clears weeks 1 / 2 / 3 around day 7 / 21 / 28 as before, income +2%). Tests cover the leftovers sale, alternate carry-over, the contract button and text, the week label and the new unlock rules. Save version 6 with a migration.

**Open follow-up:** surplus at 1 coin a unit is small on purpose; the real answer to a dead early upgrade (an extra batch a day, or multi-batch crafting) belongs to the crafting rework. Older saves that already own royal jelly keep it.
