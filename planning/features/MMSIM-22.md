# MMSIM-22 — The animated workshop, part A: the scene shell

Motivation: step 2 of the cozy loop (`docs/animated-cozy-loop-v5.md`, phase 1): a pixel-art workshop with animated mice, and a routine paced by what the scene plays. Built with placeholder sprites; the real art replaces the strips, not the code.

- IN:
  - Content tables `sprites.json` (frame size, per action: strip file, frames, fps, loop; `idle` required) and `scenes.json` (background file and size, and the actors: sprite, the crafting `step` it does, and the top-left corner of its frame in scene pixels). Setting `sceneStepMs` (1400): how long a step's mouse works. Content check: an actor's sprite and step must exist.
  - `src/view/scene.ts`: `buildSceneView` (the scene with only the mice whose step a helper does: porter fills, stirrer stirs, baker does the oven, sculptor shapes) and the pure `describeBeat(c, s, action)`: for a `craft` action, one clip per step in order (fill, stir, the quality's extra steps, shape), none for other actions.
  - **The scene is the clock.** `GameSession.peekRoutine()` names the next routine action without applying it. The UI driver (`src/ui/game.svelte.ts`) plays the clips (each mouse works in turn) and only then calls `tickRoutine()`, so the stock and coins never show a result before it is played. Actions without a clip (serving, ending a phase) keep the 350 ms beat; `prefers-reduced-motion` skips the clips.
  - `src/ui/Scene.svelte`: the background and the mice as CSS sprites (strip stepped with `steps()`), drawn at 256x144 and scaled by a whole number up to x3 (a maximum height), a fraction on a phone. It sits at the top of the workshop; mice idle (a bob) and work while their clip plays.
  - Art: the cleaned mice (`assets/mice-crops/`), the empty workshop as the background (`public/art/scenes/workshop.png`), and 4-frame idle and work strips made by `scripts/art/make_placeholder_sheets.py` (moves the upper body of each sprite by a pixel or two). Palette in `docs/art/`.
  - No State change, no save migration.
- OUT (later parts of the design doc): the ingredient-by-ingredient sequences, a counter and an evening scene, walking and customers, a speed control and skip, hiding panels as steps are automated, the thin HUD and the evening "run tomorrow" choice, Stop, hand-crafting on the scene, the real Pixellab art, a text alternative beyond the scene's label.
- Decisions: an actor's anchor is its frame's top-left corner in scene pixels (stationary workers need nothing else; walkers will need a feet point). Mice are shown when their step is automated, whether or not the routine is on, so owning the helper makes the workshop come alive. The scene is at the top of the workshop card for now; "behind the workshop" is the layout phase.
- Verified: `npm run check`, `npm test` (new `src/view/scene.test.ts`), `npm run check:content`. In the browser (dev URL with all four helpers): the mice show on the empty workshop and idle; switching the routine on plays porter, stirrer, baker, sculptor in turn for a batch; no console errors; at 375 px the scene is scaled to 307 px wide with no horizontal overflow. Not done: the design-brief screenshots and catalog were not regenerated; dark and light look not compared.
- Open decisions: whether mice should show before their helper is switched on by the routine; how long a step should take once the speed control exists; whether the scene should also appear on the afternoon screen (the counter scene).
