# MMSIM-6 — Weekly stages, errands, map strip, rival pressure (v1 scope M5)

Motivation: `docs/incremental-game-v1-scope.md` M5: weekly stages, targets, errands, map strip, rival pressure. Layout stays basic for the later design pass.

- IN:
  - **Weekly stages** (`stages.json`, 3 rows): a week is `days` days (7); reaching `target` coins of revenue earned that week opens the next stage, otherwise the same stage repeats with everything kept (no fail state). Later stages add demand (`arrivalsMult` 1 / 1.3 / 1.6) and targets 200 / 400 / 700 (placeholders). Clearing the last stage sets `completed` and play continues on it. A stage bar (all phases) shows the week's progress including today's sales, the rival warning, last week's result, and, once complete, `ending.text` if an optional pack provides it (otherwise a generic line, so preview builds work).
  - **Rival pressure**: a stage may have a `rival` (stage 2 and 3: 2 honey stolen each morning). Never pushes supply below `settings.rivalMinSupply` (2). Relieved by the errand reward stat `rivalRelief`.
  - **Errands** (`errands.json`, 3 scripted): `queen-fever` (accepting unlocks the Royal tonic recipe; hand-craft and deliver 4; reward +2 honey per day forever), `pilgrim-feast` (deliver 8 Fine honey drops; reward +1 counter space; needs the oven and stage 2), `rival-hive` (a mission: the clerk is away for 2 days, during which they cannot sell; reward removes the rival's honey theft for good; stage 2, needs the clerk). Rewards reuse the skill-tree effect system (`modifier`/`unlock`/`addStation`), so finished errands count like owned upgrades. Errand-only recipes (`errandOnly`) are never asked for by customers or contracts. Engine actions `acceptErrand` / `deliverErrand`; the Temple/Hive branch gained `errands-board` and `map` nodes (each requires The Faithful).
  - **Map strip** (evening, after buying the `map` node): a fixed-size art slot (`map-strip`, 640x120) plus place chips (3 places, revealed by stage; locked ones read "Unknown place").
  - **Save v2** with a real migration (v1 saves load with the week counter at stage 1 / day 0): verified on the actual v1 save left in the browser by M4 testing.
  - Journal: lines for "week passed" / "week missed" (`weekResult` condition) on the day after a week closes. Simulator: stage column, per-week results, bot takes errands and makes/delivers what they ask. Dev URL: `&stage=2` starts in stage N.
- OUT: random errands and minigames (v1.1), per-worker skill ("result after 1-2 days based on staff skill": the mission duration is fixed at 2 days for now), a purchasable "rank milestones" node (stages are always on: gating the main progression behind a purchase made no sense) and "rival pressure" as a purchase (it is stage-driven instead), errands touching live campaign plot in a pack (the `plot-errands` pack mechanism exists, the 3 errands are in core; moving one is moving its rows and strings), the ending text itself.
- Rules fixed here (challenge in M6): week revenue counts coins earned (not net of spending); the week is judged after its last day; a mission begins the moment it is accepted and pays out at the start of the day when its counter hits 0; errand requirements are unlock ids.
- Verified: `npm test` 80 passing (stages pass/repeat/complete, arrivals scaling, rival floor and relief, errand gating/recipe/delivery/mission/worker-away/rewards, migration from v1, journal week lines), `npm run check` 0 errors, `npm run check:content` and both builds clean. `npm run sim -- --days 70` plays through all three stages (bot clears stage 1, hovers around the stage 2 and 3 targets: tuning is M6). In the browser (FR/EN): the real v1 save migrated and showed "jour 1 / 7 · 40 / 200"; with `&stage=2` the rival warning, errand list, accepting, crafting and delivering the tonic, the mission counting down 2 -> 1 -> done, honey 4 -> 4 -> 6 as theft and rewards stacked, a missed week showing its banner and re-translating on language switch.
- Open decisions: balance of targets/rival/errand rewards; whether errands should expire; whether the stage bar belongs on every screen; real map art; the ending (pack) text; showing which unlock an errand still needs is by upgrade name only.
