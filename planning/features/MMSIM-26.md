# MMSIM-26 — The animated counter, part A: a placeholder shop to play with, and the accounting picture

Motivation: the afternoon was a list of cards while the morning is a living workshop. Before drawing anything, try the selling interaction with moving rectangles (playtest discussion, 2026-10-17).

- IN:
  - Layout (revised after the first playtest): the scene (left) and **the queue list (right)** side by side, then a stock row, then a clerk strip and the latest events. Today's board moved to a drawer (the same one as the morning). Rationale: queue position means nothing for selling (any customer can be served in any order), so the scene is ambience and the list is the interface; the goal of a selling turn is "who pays most for this", so each row shows the best payout we can offer (price+tip, from `CustomerCard.best`), the want, the least quality, the budget cap and patience in turns as pips, with a one-click **Serve** (the best offer); opening a row shows the other offers and Refuse. Rows are ranked by best payout (unservable last).
  - `src/ui/CounterScene.svelte`: a top-down **placeholder** shop, 256 x 176 scene units. A counter and the clerk rectangle at the top, the door at the bottom, a two-wide queue lane up the middle (eight visible, a "+N" badge for the rest and for those still outside), two tables on each side. Customers are coloured squares (colour per type, `customerColor.ts`) that walk in from the door and are clickable (the same selection as the list). A served customer walks to a seat and **stays for the next two turns** (a turn = a customer served or turned away; no real-time timer); a refused or departed one walks out. Only the walking is real time.
  - `QueueList.svelte`, `Serve.svelte` (rewritten), `serve.ts` (`best`, `ranked`, `stock`, `outside`), `board.ts` exports `stockTable`.
  - The accounting picture (`public/art/scenes/accounting-idle.png`, 208 x 128, from `assets/accounting-background.png`) fills the evening journal card and fades into the page under the text. `accounting.png` (with the mouse) is in place but unused: nothing shows the accounting mouse working yet.
  - String `serve.pickOne` (EN, FR).
- OUT: the satisfaction rating (plus or minus 10 % on prices, decided), real customer sprites, a waiter that walks the order to the table, the pending crowd badge ("+N at the door"), the accounting mouse mechanic, art for the counter.
- Decisions: every customer on the counter has a slot, so with the counter at its maximum the second row is full; the scene does not hold the pending customers (they are not at the counter yet).
- Verified: `npm run check`, `npm test`. In the browser (`?endgame=1`): the scene shows the whole queue, a click opens the order, serving sends the customer to a table and the line refills, the journal picture loads. Not checked: phone width, dark mode, the routine's serving animation over many customers.
