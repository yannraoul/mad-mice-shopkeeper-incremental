# MMSIM-14 — The content fill: all four ingredients, fifteen candies, the real final quest

Motivation: MMSIM-11 built the ingredient, quality and alternate mechanics with only honey and sunflower seeds; the playtest asked for "all the main ingredients first". Design in `docs/recipes-and-ingredients-v2.md`; checked by `src/content/recipes.test.ts`.

- IN:
  - **Milk and blueberry**, 2 a day each, with their rare alternates **Cream from Stumpsville** and **Cursed blackberry** (15% of the delivered base units once unlocked, sold for 5 at closing like the other alternates). New upgrades **More milk** (4 levels) and **More blueberries** (4 levels), kitchen branch, opened by the matching ingredient.
  - **Every combination of the four base ingredients is a candy: 15**, auto-opened once their ingredients are. The 12 new: Toffee, Berrycorice, Honey fudge, Honey-berry gums, Seed praline, Berry crunch, Berry cream chews, Golden truffle, Meadow bar, Berry cloud, Cheesecake bites and **The Sweet Tooth** (all four). Prices follow one formula (ingredient values 5 / 6 / 7 / 8, +25% per extra ingredient; a content test keeps them within 8% of it). Fine adds a unit of the first ingredient, Excellent adds one of the first and one of the second (singles 2x and 3x of their ingredient); Perfect is the plain recipe with every ingredient replaced by its alternate. 16 EN/FR names, placeholder art for the 12 candies and 4 ingredients.
  - **Ingredient quests** (errands, EN/FR): the scarecrow mice (stage 3: 6 Fine candy canes, +2 sunflower a day), the Stumpsville dairy cart (stage 4: 8 sunflower brittles, opens milk, +1 a day), the bramble thicket (stage 5: 6 Fine nougats, opens blueberries, +1 a day). The alternates open after the magic cauldron: the Stumpsville trade route (stage 6: 6 Fine honey fudges, cream) and the cursed bramble (stage 7: 6 Fine honey-berry gums, blackberries), next to the hive's gratitude and the dark seed garden that were already there. A new map place, Stumpsville road (stage 6, +10 coins a day).
  - **The final quest is real**: the offering to Sweet Tooth is now 3 Perfect Sweet Tooth candies, which needs all four alternates in the cauldron on the same morning (jelly, dark seed, cream, blackberry), hence luck and patience; it needs all four alternate unlocks.
  - **Customers**: the merchant now wants a candy with 2 or more ingredients (it was 1 until now) and has a budget of 45, so the pairs and triples have somebody to sell to.
  - Craft screen: with more than four ingredients the shelf takes two columns on desktop.
  - Stage targets were raised to 240 / 520 / 1150 / 1900 / 2800 / 3500 / 4000 / 4300 / 4500: with 15 candies and the bigger merchant budget the competent bot earns about 580 a day (425 before).
- OUT: customer types rich enough for the Sweet Tooth (price 46 against the merchant's 45 budget, so it sells to nobody but the final quest); events; the four-branch tree; the crafting rework (MMSIM-15 and after).
- Decisions taken where the design left room:
  - The base ingredient quests are "deliver candies" errands that need the errands board; a base ingredient opens through its quest, not an upgrade (except sunflower, kept as the Sunflower patch).
  - Quest candies are chosen so a player has them when the quest opens, and so the quests vary (canes, brittles, nougats, fudges, gums).
  - Alternates are sold at closing, so the final quest is a matter of the day all four arrive together (about a 1 in 7 chance a day with 4 to 6 units of each base ingredient): the bot delivers it a few days after the last stage opens.
- Verified: `npm run check`, `npm test` (195), `npm run check:content`, `npm run build`, `npm run balance`: competent bot clears one stage a week and delivers the final quest on day 57 to 71, average bot around day 60 to 76 (2 of 40 not by day 84); browser: the Craft screen with all eight ingredients.
- Open decisions: the rare alternates and the "same morning" rule may feel like a wait for luck: a carry-over, a higher chance or an upgrade (`altChance:<id>` is ready for it) could soften it; late income is demand limited (about 4000 a week) and unsold stock is large (about 160 candies on opening); Excellent and Perfect still only pay through customers' minimum qualities and tips; French candy names are mine, check them.
