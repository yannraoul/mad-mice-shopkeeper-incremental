# MMSIM-11 — Recipes, ingredients and qualities v2

Motivation: playtest 1 (first "more honey" did nothing, sunflower seeds had no use, every recipe was honey, quality was a global rule). Design in `docs/recipes-and-ingredients-v2.md`; cravings (MMSIM-12), the week chain (MMSIM-13) and the content fill (MMSIM-14) are separate.

- IN:
  - **Ingredients** are content with an `unlock`; only unlocked ones are delivered, shown on the shelf and listed. Each base ingredient has one rare **alternate** (`alternateOf`, `chance`): once the alternate is unlocked, every delivered base unit has that chance (15% for now, modifier stat `altChance:<id>` ready for reputation) to arrive as the alternate instead, so alternates trickle in and are never guaranteed. Draws only happen once an alternate is unlocked, so seeds of earlier games are untouched until then.
  - **Content now**: honey (start; alternate royal jelly, unlocked by the queen's errand reward) and sunflower seed (Sunflower patch node; alternate dark sunflower seed, Dark garden node). Daily supply: honey 3 (one per batch), sunflower 3, `honey-yield` and new `sunflower-yield` nodes.
  - **Candies**: a recipe opens by itself once every plain ingredient is unlocked (`isCandyUnlocked`); `recipe-2`/`recipe-3` nodes and the royal tonic are gone. Candy cane (renamed from Honey drop, id `candy-cane`, 1 honey, 5), Sunflower brittle (1 sunflower, 6), Sunflower nougat (1 honey + 1 sunflower, 14). Per-candy `extra` units per quality (Fine and Excellent add base units; singles are 1x, 2x, 3x).
  - **Qualities**: Plain, Fine (oven, x1.6), **Excellent** (new node Pulling machine, extra "Pull" step, x2.2), **Perfect** (x3.5): `alternates: true` means the plain quantities with every ingredient replaced by its alternate; unlocked by the new **Magic cauldron** errand (deliver 4 Excellent candy canes, needs the errands board and the pulling machine); extra "Whisper" step. Contracts never ask for Perfect (alternates are not guaranteed).
  - **Queen errand** now asks for 4 Fine candy canes (needs the oven), rewards +2 honey a day, the bees and royal jelly. Bees want candy cane or nougat (until cravings).
  - Content validation: an alternate has exactly one base and no supply of its own, every ingredient used by a recipe has exactly one alternate, every ingredient and quality unlock id is granted by a node or an errand.
  - **Save version 4** with a migration (Honey drop to Candy cane, `recipe-2` to `sunflower-patch`/`sunflower`, tonic and toffee stock, contracts and customers dropped, stray dark seeds removed).
  - View: shelf and supplies list only unlocked ingredients; recipe cards show the cost at the chosen quality; the step track shows Fill, Stir, Bake, Pull, Whisper, Shape and dims what the quality does not use. Strings EN/FR, three placeholder SVG slots, catalog regenerated.
  - Balance tooling: bots' upgrade order updated; "honey unused" became "ingredients unused" (all unlocked base ingredients).
- OUT: milk, blueberry and their alternates, the 12 other candies, the Sweet Tooth, per-ingredient quests (MMSIM-14); cravings, happiness and tips (MMSIM-12); the stage chain and the final goal (MMSIM-13); reputation upgrades; the four-branch tree.
- Decisions taken where the design left room:
  - The pulling machine is a **node that unlocks Excellent and adds a "Pull" step** (like the oven), not a separate station instance with its own batches: stations are cauldrons with batch counters, and a step needs none. Say if you want a true second station.
  - The Magic cauldron quest requires the pulling machine instead of "all cauldron upgrades bought" (errands can only require unlock ids).
  - Alternate chance is 15% a unit, one number for both alternates, to be tuned.
  - Ids: `candy-cane` replaces `honey-drop` everywhere (code, content, art file, tests).
- Verified: `npm run check`, `npm test` (163), `npm run check:content`; `npm run balance`: smart bot clears week 1 / 2 / 3 around day 7 / 21 / 49, casual later, every upgrade matters in `--ablate` except the map and the clerk (honey-yield is -24% revenue without it, the original complaint); browser: Perfect shows alternate costs and "Not enough royal jelly: this batch needs 1, you have 0".
- Open decisions: ingredients are now **not** the late bottleneck (about half go unused; batches and customers limit the shop), so quality only pays once cravings and tips exist (MMSIM-12); week 2 clears about a week earlier than before (21 against 28); the casual bot often fails week 3 (it crafts the best quality first and burns ingredients); the bots never make Perfect.
