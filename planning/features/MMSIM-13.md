# MMSIM-13 — The stage chain: nine stages, things that open, passive income, the final quest

Motivation: the campaign was three stages and the last one was "the end"; a missed week changed nothing and the whole game was over in 28 days (a playtest save cleared it that fast). Design in `docs/stages-and-progression-v2.md`.

- IN:
  - **Nine stages**, each a 7 day stage with a revenue target (`stages.json`: 240, 700, 1300, 1950, 2600, 2950, 3050, 3150, 3250), more customers per stage up to x3, the rival hive in stages 2 and 3 as before. Time is counted in **calendar weeks and days** ("stage 3 · week 5 · day 2 / 7", also in contract deadlines: "by week 2, day 3"); a stage is what you complete. Names in EN/FR for all nine.
  - **Passing a stage opens things** by `minStage` (0-based stage index) on upgrades, errands and places; the strip says on the first day "New this stage: Second cauldron, Hive bond, Map strip, Reputation, The pilgrims' feast, Millpond." (built from the data, so it cannot drift). Locked upgrades are visible with "Opens at stage 6." Schedule: stage 2 sunflower patch, errands board, contract board, the queen's quest and the rival errand; stage 3 second cauldron, hive bond, map, reputation, the pilgrims' feast, the first place; stage 4 pulling machine; stage 5 magic cauldron quest; stage 6 dark seed garden and the hive's gratitude; stage 9 the final quest.
  - **Easy mode** (`settings.retryRelief` 10% a miss, up to `retryReliefMax` 30%): a missed week repeats the same stage with a lower target; passing resets it. The strip shows the lowered target.
  - **Passive income**: places on the map pay `income` coins every morning once open (3, 5, 8, 12, 18; two new places, Market stall and Roadside inn). Needs the map upgrade. Counts as revenue; the evening report shows "Map income".
  - **Reputation**: new shop upgrade, 5 levels, +6%, rounded up, on what customers pay (and so on tips) per level (`priceBonus`; 4% rounded to nothing on 5 coin candies, see MMSIB-13).
  - **The final quest** (`final: true` on an errand): "The offering to Sweet Tooth", 3 Perfect sunflower nougats (placeholder until the Sweet Tooth exists in MMSIM-14), opens at stage 9, needs the errands board, the magic cauldron, royal jelly and the dark seed garden. Delivering it completes the game. Passing the last stage no longer ends anything: weeks go on.
  - Save version 7 (stages remember misses, days record income, `completed` now means the final quest is delivered); balance tooling reports the day the final quest is delivered; the ending pack text was updated.
- OUT: stage rewards that are direct bonuses (the schema has no `rewards` yet: everything opens through `minStage`); events; the four-branch tree; the crafting rework (autofill, multi-batch, minigames).
- Decisions taken where the design left room:
  - Nine stages of seven days each, so a stage and a calendar week line up exactly; a missed week just repeats, which keeps the calendar honest. If stages ever get a different length the week label needs a stage start day.
  - The first place needs the map upgrade, which opens at stage 3.
  - Targets were set from the casual bot's weekly revenue (about 100%), so a competent player rarely misses and an average one misses about three weeks a run.
  - Quest gating is by stage index, not by stage-specific unlock ids: simpler to read in the data and to list in the UI.
- Verified: `npm run check`, `npm test` (188), `npm run check:content`, `npm run build`, `npm run balance`: the competent bot clears the stages on day 7, 14, ... 63 and delivers the final quest on day 57 to 64; the casual bot finishes around day 71 (4 of 40 not by day 84); browser: strip text, "New this stage", locked reasons, evening report and the map list.
- Open decisions: the numbers are bot tuned (revenue per day now 340 to 400, up from 330 with the places, reputation and bigger crowds), the plateau is demand limited (about 3000 a week) so late targets sit close to it; stages 4 to 8 open little (waiting on milk and blueberry in MMSIM-14) so the middle may feel empty; leftovers and map income are a small share of income; the old three stage saves land on stage 3 of 9.
