# MMSIM-7 — Balance pass and preview-build pipeline (v1 scope M6)

Motivation: `docs/incremental-game-v1-scope.md` M6: balance with the simulator, personal playtest, preview-build pipeline. The personal playtest is the author's: `planning/PLAYTEST.md` is the checklist, with the pacing targets and a "feels wrong -> turn this knob" table.

- IN:
  - **Balance tooling** (`npm run balance -- [--seeds 40] [--days 84] [--bot smart|casual|cheap|both] [--ablate]`): many seeded runs summarised as stage-clear days (p10/median/p90, runs that never clear), missed weeks, revenue by week, first purchase day of each upgrade, customers lost/refused, unused honey, stock on opening. Three bots as yardsticks (`src/sim/`): `smart` (priority upgrade order, restocks the least stocked recipe, serves the impatient first), `casual` (same planning, serves in queue order), `cheap` (the old naive rotation bot, a lower bound). `--ablate` removes each upgrade in turn and reports the revenue change and final-stage delay for the competent bot.
  - **Guard tests** (`src/sim/balance.test.ts`, 10 fixed seeds x 98 days): competent player clears stage 1 by day 14, stage 2 between day 14 and 42, stage 3 between day 35 and 63; an average player also finishes (median <= day 77); a careless one still passes stage 2; income keeps growing; every upgrade is bought; no mountain of stock or wasted honey; few customers lost. If a content edit leaves these bands, a test fails and the change must be deliberate.
  - **Tuning** (measured, not guessed): first measurement showed the game was demand-bound (about 600 unsold candies, revenue plateau 130/day), a 3-4 week campaign, and the whole tree bought by day 17. Fixes: a new `arrivals` stat (flat customers per day) raised by The Faithful, Hive bond and the pilgrims' feast; stage demand 1 / 1.5 / 2.8; targets 240 / 800 / 1900 coins per week; upgrade costs re-scaled to income (20 to 1100 coins); customers less patient (Faithful 6, Traveler 4, Merchant 2); Hive bond now brings visitors (+1 honey, +2 customers/day per level); counter space gives a real benefit (see below).
  - **Design bug found by the measurements**: counter space was a trap. Waiting customers only lose patience while at the counter, so a bigger counter exposed more people to the drain and revenue fell when you bought it. Now `settings.customersPerSpace` (4) caps how many customers the shop welcomes per day (counter space x 4), so stages 2 and 3 need it.
  - **Preview pipeline**: `npm run build:preview` builds without packs, then `scripts/verify-preview.ts` (`npm run verify:preview`) reads the actual `dist-preview/` and fails if any text value of any pack appears in it. `scripts/preview.test.ts` runs a real Vite build with and without packs over a temporary content copy that has a secret marker pack and asserts the marker is absent from the preview and present in the full build. The `ending` pack now holds a placeholder `ending.text` (EN/FR) shown on the stage bar once the last stage is cleared. `readContentFiles` / `contentPlugin` take an optional content directory.
- Result (40 seeds, 112 days): competent player clears stages on day 7 / 28 / 49 (p90 56); average player 7 / 28 / 56 (p90 70); careless player passes stages 1 and 2 (about day 35) but never stage 3. Competent income about 40/day in week 1 rising to about 285/day by week 7. Without any single upgrade the competent player loses 4 to 38% revenue, except automation/convenience nodes by design: clerk and map are about 0, the second cauldron about 15% for this bot (the clerk and auto cauldron mostly save the human taps the bot does not pay for).
- OUT: a human-calibrated difficulty (only the author can judge); an economy sink for coins after the last stage; per-candy demand (customers want one candy each); the bots do not use the forecast; real sound/animation pacing.
- Verified: `npm test` 93 passing (new: 7 balance guards, 1 real-build preview test), `npm run check` 0 errors, `npm run check:content` clean, `npm run build` and `npm run build:preview` succeed and verify says "2 pack text value(s) checked, none found"; grep confirms the ending text is in `dist/` and not in `dist-preview/`. In the browser: stage 3 dev URL shows the new costs/descriptions, the rival warning, and the ending placeholder once `completed` is true; no console errors.
- Open decisions: all numbers are the tuning of a bot, not of a person: expect to adjust after the author's playtest (use `planning/PLAYTEST.md`); whether the campaign should be longer than about 7 weeks; the final stage has no goal beyond its target (ending pack text is a placeholder); `cheap` bot cannot finish, which is by design but shows that careless crafting is heavily punished (the forecast is the tool against that).
